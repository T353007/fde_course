# Section 3. Revenue and cash flow

Minimum annual operating revenue for a term loan is 300,000 dollars. This went
up from 250,000 in the 2024 policy. Operating revenue means money the business
earned from customers.

Do not count these as operating revenue:
- transfers between accounts held by the same business or owner
- proceeds from any loan, line of credit, or merchant cash advance
- owner capital contributions
- refunds and chargeback reversals
- one time asset sales

# Section 4. Debt service coverage

Debt service coverage ratio must be at least 1.25 for a term loan and 1.15 for a
line of credit. Calculate it from operating revenue, not total deposits.

# Section 6. Documentation

Require three months of business bank statements. Six months if the business is
under two years old, or if any single deposit is more than 40 percent of the
period total.

# Section 9. Time in business

Minimum time in business is 24 months for a term loan and 12 months for a line
of credit. Equipment financing has no minimum when the equipment secures the
loan.

# Section 12. Existing debt from other lenders

If bank statements show loan proceeds from another lender, the underwriter must
obtain the payoff amount and monthly payment before decisioning. This applies
even when the other lender is not on the credit report yet.
