# Section 3. Revenue and cash flow

Minimum annual operating revenue for a term loan is 350,000 dollars.

Operating revenue is defined in Section 3.1 and excludes transfers, loan
proceeds, owner contributions, refunds, and asset sales.

# Section 3.1 Definition of operating revenue

Operating revenue is the total of credits that represent payment for goods or
services delivered by the business. Payment processor payouts count. Deposits
from a lender do not count, including deposits from a competing lender.

# Section 4. Debt service coverage

Debt service coverage ratio must be at least 1.30 for a term loan and 1.20 for a
line of credit.

# Section 6. Documentation

Require six months of business bank statements for all products.

# Section 12. Existing debt from other lenders

If bank statements show loan proceeds from another lender, obtain the payoff
amount and monthly payment before decisioning, and record the lender name in the
decision reason codes.
