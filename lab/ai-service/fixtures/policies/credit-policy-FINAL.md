# Section 3. Revenue and cash flow

Minimum annual operating revenue for a term loan is 200,000 dollars.

Total deposits may be used as a proxy for operating revenue when statements are
incomplete. Underwriter discretion applies.

# Section 4. Debt service coverage

Target debt service coverage ratio is 1.15.

# Section 6. Documentation

Two months of bank statements is acceptable for repeat borrowers.
