# Section 3. Revenue and cash flow

Minimum annual operating revenue for a term loan is 300,000 dollars.

Do not count transfers between accounts held by the same business or owner. Do
not count proceeds from any loan or merchant cash advance.

# Section 4. Debt service coverage

Debt service coverage ratio must be at least 1.25 for a term loan and 1.15 for a
line of credit.

# Section 9. Time in business

Minimum time in business is 24 months for a term loan and 12 months for a line
of credit.

# Appendix B. Reason code list

DSCR_LOW, REV_BELOW_MIN, TIB_SHORT, DOCS_INCOMPLETE, OTHER_LENDER_DEBT
