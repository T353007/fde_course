# Section 1. Scope

This addendum covers the state economic development grants program administered
on behalf of the agency. Grants are not credit products. Nothing in credit policy
applies unless this addendum says it does.

# Section 2. Award decisions

Award decisions are made by the agency review panel, not by Northstar. Northstar
performs intake, eligibility screening, and disbursement only.

# Section 3. Eligibility screening

Screen for entity registration, county of operation, and headcount. Do not screen
on revenue, credit score, or debt service coverage. Applying credit criteria to a
grant application is a program compliance violation.

# Section 4. Adverse action

Grant denials are not adverse action under ECOA because no credit was requested.
Do not send a credit denial notice for a grant decline.

# Section 7. Reporting

Quarterly reporting to the agency is due 15 days after quarter end and includes
applicant county, headcount, and award amount.
