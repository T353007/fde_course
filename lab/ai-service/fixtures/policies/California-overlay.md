# Section 1. Scope

This overlay applies to Cascade Funding applications only. Where it conflicts
with base credit policy, this overlay wins.

# Section 2. Disclosure

California commercial financing disclosure is required before any offer is
presented. The disclosure must show total dollar cost and annualized rate.

# Section 3. Revenue and cash flow

Minimum annual operating revenue for a term loan is 400,000 dollars for
California borrowers. This is higher than base policy.

# Section 5. Pricing

Maximum annualized rate for a California term loan is 24 percent. Broker
compensation is capped at 3 percent of funded amount.

# Section 8. Time in business

Minimum time in business is 36 months for California borrowers.
