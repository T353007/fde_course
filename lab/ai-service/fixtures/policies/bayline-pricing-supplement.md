# Section 1. Scope

This supplement applies to Bayline Capital applications only. Bayline runs the
same policy engine as Northstar Capital with different pricing. Do not quote
these numbers to any other brand.

# Section 5. Pricing

Maximum annualized rate for a Bayline term loan is 31 percent. Origination fee
is 4.5 percent of funded amount. Broker compensation is capped at 5 percent,
which is higher than the Northstar cap.

# Section 5.2 Rate tiers

Tier A, 18 to 22 percent, requires debt service coverage above 1.50.
Tier B, 22 to 27 percent, requires debt service coverage above 1.25.
Tier C, 27 to 31 percent, requires debt service coverage above 1.15 and a
personal guarantee from every owner with 20 percent or more.

# Section 6. Renewal pricing

Bayline renewals price one tier better than the original loan if the borrower has
made twelve consecutive on time payments.
