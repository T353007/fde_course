# Section 1. Scope

This overlay applies to SBA 7(a) applications. It wins over every other policy
document, including tenant overlays.

# Section 2. Eligibility

The business must meet SBA size standards for its NAICS code. The business must
operate for profit and be based in the United States.

# Section 3. Revenue and cash flow

There is no minimum operating revenue floor for SBA 7(a). Repayment ability is
assessed from historical cash flow and projections together.

# Section 4. Debt service coverage

Debt service coverage ratio must be at least 1.15, measured on a global basis
including owner personal debt.

# Section 6. Documentation

Require three years of business tax returns, personal tax returns for every
owner with 20 percent or more, and SBA Form 1919 and 1920.

# Section 7. Credit elsewhere

Document that credit is not available elsewhere on reasonable terms. This is an
SBA requirement and there is no exception.
