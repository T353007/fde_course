## The meeting

Marcus wants to ship an agent that can decline applications end to end with no human click, because "that is how we hit 70 percent." Ada is not in the room. Doug is on vacation until Monday. Jordan says the board deck already has the autonomous agent slide.

## Deliverables

1. What you say in the meeting in under two minutes.
2. What you say to Jordan after.
3. What you write to Doug so Monday is not a surprise.
4. The smallest safe alternative that still moves cycle time.
5. The line you will not cross, stated without drama.

:::spoiler{label="Answer key"}
Lead with Dale's goal (speed / deals), not with "agents are dangerous."

Name the regulated consequence of autonomous decline (adverse action clock, explainability, fair lending). Offer a copilot that clears document wait and rework, which is where the days actually are. Ask to pull the board slide or relabel it "assisted decisioning." Do not humiliate Marcus in public. Give him a better slide.

Hard line: no write tool that changes application state without an authorized human confirmation that is meaningful, plus audit.

Score 4 if you keep the room, protect the customer, and still say no to the unsafe part.
:::
