## The room

Dale, Priya, Marcus, Hank. You have twenty minutes. Your slice has been live for five weeks.

Facts you may use:

- Document wait median down from 5.1 days to 2.4 days on the pilot cohort
- Rework rate 63% to 41% on the same cohort
- Eval overall 96%, loan proceeds slice 81% after the hybrid pipeline (was 68%)
- AI spend this month $31k after routing fixes (was on track for $91k)
- Adoption week 1/2/3 was 67/48/29 until the click path was cut from six screens to two. Now 71%
- One confidentiality near-miss on tenant retrieval, fixed, disclosed to Priya and Yuki
- Redwood Bank wants a pilot

## Deliverables

Write the talk track as timed sections:

1. Opening (2 min): the outcome in Dale's language
2. Evidence (6 min): what moved, what did not
3. Risk (4 min): remaining risk, including the near-miss
4. Money (3 min): cost and ROI without fake precision
5. Ask (3 min): next step and what you need
6. Appendix only if asked: accuracy slices, architecture one-pager

:::spoiler{label="Answer key"}
Dale cares about deals and cycle time. Lead with days saved and rework, not model accuracy.

Name adoption as an engineering problem you owned, not a training problem for Hank's team.

Near-miss: factual, no panic, what changed in the control.

Ask: expand pilot cohort, keep autonomous decline off, decide Redwood only after Northstar adoption holds for two more weeks.

Fail if you open with 96% accuracy or a component diagram.
:::
